Caveats

  • Regional pay at 15 months comes from Luminate's table of HESA data and is labelled an average. HESA's own regional table could not be downloaded, so treat small gaps between regions with care.
  • Earnings five years on (DfE LEO) cover graduates of English universities only, include part-time workers, and come from tax records.
  • Indeed's index counts all job postings, not only graduate roles. It is seasonally adjusted and Indeed revises past values.
  • Apprenticeship starts are counted differently in each nation: academic-year starts in England, funded Modern Apprenticeship starts in Scotland, programmes started in Wales, and all programmes in Northern Ireland.
  • ISE and High Fliers survey large employers. Their changes describe the graduate schemes of those employers, not the whole market.
  • HESA's unemployed share is self-reported and is not an unemployment rate.
